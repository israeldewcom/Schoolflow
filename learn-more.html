How SchoolFlow works

SchoolFlow replaces the exercise books, loose receipts and spreadsheets that most schools run on. Here is what it does, who uses it, and how to get your school started.

Start free this term See pricing

What your school gets

Fees and receipts

Know what has been paid, and what has not

Set the fees for each class and term once. Every student gets an invoice, and balances update as payments come in. Staff record payments, but nothing counts until you approve it.

  • Balances per student, per class and for the whole school
  • Numbered receipts you can print or save as a PDF
  • Every payment waits for your approval
Report cards

Report cards for a whole class in one go

Teachers enter scores once. SchoolFlow builds the report cards with your school logo and colours, so there is no retyping at the end of term.

  • Pick a ready-made layout or upload your own template
  • Teacher and principal comments on every card
  • Print them or share them as PDFs
Lesson notes

Lesson notes that do not get lost on WhatsApp

Teachers can draft lesson notes with AI help and submit them from their phone. The head teacher reviews each one and approves it or sends it back.

  • See which notes are waiting and which are approved
  • Teachers keep all their notes in one place
  • You always review the draft before it counts
Boarding

Run your hostels without a register book

Set up hostels and rooms, allocate beds, and take roll call at night. Hostel managers get their own login that shows only their residents.

  • Beds filled and beds free in every room
  • Night roll call with present, absent and exeat
  • A separate login for each hostel manager

And the everyday basics

Attendance

Teachers mark attendance from a phone, and the dashboard shows who is present today.

Timetables

Build a weekly timetable for every class so teachers always know where they should be.

Parent and student portals

Parents sign in with their phone number to see their child's results and fee balance. Students sign in with their admission number.

SMS reminders

Send fee reminders to parents by SMS, using credits you top up when you need them.

Your school website

Your school gets a simple website you can edit, with a link and a QR code to share.

Works on your phone

Open it in a browser or install it like an app. If the network drops, you can still view saved information.

Questions schools ask

Do I need to install anything?

No. SchoolFlow runs in the browser on a phone or laptop. You can also install it on your phone like an app.

How much does it cost?

You can pay monthly, per term, or per student at ₦600 per student per term. Paying for the whole year on the per-student plan saves 10%. You can start free this term with no card. See the full pricing.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer. You send the payment, enter the transaction reference in the app, and we activate your plan once we confirm it.

Who can see my school's records?

Only the people you add, and each person sees only what their role allows. Parents see only their own children, and every payment needs your approval before it counts.

What if the network is poor at my school?

You can still open saved information when the network drops, but saving changes needs a connection. Most schools record payments and attendance when they are online.

Can I bring my existing student list?

Yes. Students can be imported in bulk. If you get stuck, message us on WhatsApp and we will help you.
